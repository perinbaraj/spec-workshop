# Specification requirements review

The participant checks items only after confirming supporting specification
content. A checked list alone is not human approval.

- [ ] The feature goal, scope and exclusions match the binding contract.
- [ ] Prioritized stories and observable acceptance scenarios cover the need.
- [ ] Privacy, member isolation, input safety and accessibility apply throughout.
- [ ] Defaults, validation, boundary, empty and failure cases are specified.
- [ ] Success criteria are measurable without claiming unobserved results.
- [ ] Assumptions are grounded and unresolved questions/placeholders are removed.
- [ ] The specification is internally consistent and ready for human review.
